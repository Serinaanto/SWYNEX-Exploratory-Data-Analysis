# Netflix Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Project Overview
This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a cleaned Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset. The objective is to understand the content available on Netflix by analysing different attributes, identifying patterns, and discovering useful insights through statistical analysis and visualizations.

## Dataset Information
- Dataset: Netflix Movies and TV Shows
- Total Records: 8,807
- Total Columns: 12
- Dataset File: `cleaned_netflix.csv`

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Google Colab
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Analysis Performed

The following analyses were conducted:

1. Distribution of Movies and TV Shows.
2. Netflix content released over the years.
3. Top 10 countries producing Netflix content.
4. Top 10 genres available on Netflix.
5. Distribution of movie durations.
6. Analysis of content ratings.
7. Netflix content addition trends over the years.

## Key Insights

1. **Movies vs TV Shows:** Movies account for approximately 69.6% of the dataset, while TV Shows account for 30.4%.

2. **Release Year Analysis:** The year 2018 recorded the highest number of content releases, with 1,147 titles.

3. **Country Analysis:** The United States leads with 3,689 titles, followed by India with 1,046 titles.

4. **Genre Analysis:** International Movies is the most frequently listed category, with 2,752 titles, followed by Dramas with 2,427 titles.

5. **Movie Duration:** The average movie duration is approximately 99.58 minutes, with a median duration of 98 minutes.

6. **Content Ratings:** TV-MA is the most common content rating, with 3,207 titles, followed by TV-14 with 2,160 titles.

7. **Content Addition Trends:** Netflix added the highest number of titles in 2019, with 2,016 titles.

## Conclusion

This exploratory data analysis helped identify important patterns in Netflix's content library. The analysis revealed the distribution of content types, popular genres, leading countries, movie durations, content ratings, and yearly content trends.

The project provided practical experience in data analysis, data visualization, and extracting meaningful insights from a real-world dataset.
